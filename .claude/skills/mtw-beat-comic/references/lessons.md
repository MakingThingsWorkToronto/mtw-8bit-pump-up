# Beat Comic lessons (each one cost time once)

Homepage B, 2026-09-26. Keep adding: occurrence, cause, repair, and the check that now catches it.

## Beat mapping

1. **No single detector is trusted.** On "Aphelion" an all-band onset comb read 75 BPM (a 3:4 lock); autocorrelation read 100. The first hand-measured downbeat (0.1625 s) was off by about two-thirds of a beat; the all-band comb, the kick-band comb and a beat-contrast criterion all agreed on 0.565 s, and two of three silence re-entries sat on that grid. Silence re-entries alone disagreed with each other (a drop can be a pickup). `analyze-song.mjs` now takes tempo from autocorrelation, snaps to the half BPM producers use, and takes the phase from beat-to-beat loudness contrast. **Check:** play the page with `?beat=tick` and listen before cutting a story to the map.
2. **Read the per-bar table, not only the lists.** The structure that matters is musical: the drop after the intro stop, the breakdown, the accent on the last beat of every other phrase (31, 63, 95, 127, 159 in Aphelion). The heuristic phrase phase can disagree with what you hear; your ear and the table win.
3. **One pass of the song is the loop.** Two story loops per song pass forced a restart mid-song; a story as long as the song (192 beats) loops with the track, and the song's final silence becomes the "TO BE CONTINUED." gag.
4a. **"The music does not start automatically on Vercel"** (Matt, 2026-09-26). Browsers refuse audible autoplay on a first visit; it had played locally only because Matt's browser knew the site. The deployed reel waited silently for a gesture behind a faint, paused equalizer and folded its tag after ten seconds, so nothing said sound was available. Repair: the tag stays open with a pulsing "Tap for sound" while refused, the speaker starts the music instead of muting, and the unlock listens to every activation event (`pointerdown`, `pointerup`, `touchend`, `click`, `keydown`; a touch `touchstart` does not count). **Check:** `beat-comic-autoplay.mjs` (gesture policy forced on, desktop click and phone tap). The phone-tap emulation passed before the repair too, so a real phone remains the evidence for touch.
4. **Music starts late.** Autoplay can be refused or loading can stall (an automation window suspended media entirely). When playback begins, seek the music to where the story is; do not jump the story back.

## Rendering

5. **An opacity or transform on an ancestor isolates `mix-blend-mode`.** Holograms screened onto the teal band showed as dark boxes until the fade moved onto the images themselves and the layers lost their stacking contexts. The flat comic no longer blends, but keep the rule for any blended layer.
6. **A `z-index` on a text block paints it above a later, unlayered canvas.** The page cuts slid under the headings until the cut canvas got its own `z-index`.
7. **Dither turns a flat fill into a checkerboard.** Posterizing with a Bayer dither made the panel colour (L≈0.12) flicker between two levels in letterbox bands; auto-levels then re-lifted it. Fix: fill letterbox black and let auto-levels map it to the darkest flat colour.
8. **Small panels need finer pixels.** A 6 px pixel made "14 WAYS IN." unreadable in a quarter-size cell; the final pixel is now about one-fortieth of the panel height (3 px minimum). SFX sublines need their own finer final pixel.
9. **Portrait screens transpose pages, except full-width rows.** Transposing turns four tall panels into four stacked wide ones (good) but turns full-width type rows into slivers (bad): a page with any full-width panel keeps its layout. Tall panels stack the mark above the type.
10. **Slabs near the edge are clamped** so the whole sprite stays on the page (a phone cut "TO BE CONTINUED." in half).
11. **Per-tile colour strings are slow.** Canvas tiles are bucketed by colour and quantized alpha, a few dozen fills a frame. Render cost stays 1-4 ms a frame (7-30 ms on a first seek while images decode).

## Behaviour

12. **The safety fallback must not fire on a slow load.** A fixed 5 s "engine never arrived" timer disarmed the reel when three frames loaded at once; it now waits for the page `load` event plus 1.5 s.
13. **Anything that moves the page enters it.** A test tool scrolls by script, which `overflow: hidden` does not stop; a scroll listener snaps to the top and collapses. The first wheel gesture's inertia is swallowed so the reader lands on the hero.
14. **Collapse lands on the resting box** of the hero panel (its reveal holds it offset and scaled), holds the framed grid until the real panel starts its reveal, then fades over it.
15. **Automation screenshots can lie.** A window moved to a display with a different pixel ratio returned crops; use `beat-comic-frames.mjs` (headless, fixed viewport, device scale 1) for evidence.
