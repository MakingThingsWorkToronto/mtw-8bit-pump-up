## The song under it

"Aphelion" by Zambolino (Free To Use; https://freetouse.com/music/zambolino/aphelion). 100 BPM, 0.6 s a beat, first downbeat 0.565 s. Quiet intro to beat 8; **dead stop 8-9, the band drops back in on 10**; groove with an accent on the last beat of every other phrase (**31, 63, 95, 127, 159**) plus **52, 74, 106, 170**; breakdown 76-83, **dead stop 84, back on 85**; **dead stop 147-148, back on 149**; **the song ends on 190**. One pass is 192 beats (115.2 s); the story loops with it.
